# ADHD Winner

(Anciennement « Bravo ».)

To-do list en jeu pour les personnes TDAH : récompenses immédiates, défis du jour, agenda avec alarmes, minuteur focus, coach IA Nour et bilan de la semaine.

Application distincte de My Style.

## Nour's gestures
Nour reacts on every page: waves hello (Today, Coach), turns a page when you change tab, points at the Calendar, breathes calmly on Focus, cheers when you win, and puts a finger on her chin with a thought bubble while she thinks.
To use real animated clips later, add the video files (e.g. `nour-think.mp4`) and list them in `NOUR_CLIPS` in `index.html`.
